How the CPR score works

CPR stands for calories-to-protein ratio. Divide grams of protein by calories, then compare the result with 1 g of protein for every 10 calories. A chicken breast scores far above 100%, most bread scores below 40%, and a balanced high-protein day lands between 70% and 100%.
