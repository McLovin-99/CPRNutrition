Protein per calorie

Two tablespoons of peanut butter: 7 g of protein. Six and a half cups of broccoli: 17 g.

Both are 200 calories. The difference is protein per calorie, and it decides whether you can hit a protein goal without going over on calories. A cup of diced chicken breast gets you 38 g.
