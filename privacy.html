Introduction

CPR Nutrition ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the CPR Nutrition mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials (password is encrypted and never stored in plain text)
  • Profile information you choose to provide

2. Health and Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, we may access the following data from Apple HealthKit:

  • Step count
  • Active energy burned
  • Workout data

Important HealthKit Disclosure: We only read HealthKit data to display your fitness metrics within the App. We do NOT:

  • Share HealthKit data with third parties
  • Use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Sell HealthKit data to data brokers or any other entities

HealthKit data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Nutrition and Tracking Data

We collect data you manually enter, including:

  • Food logs and meal entries
  • Calorie and macronutrient information
  • Custom recipes and saved meals
  • Supplement tracking data
  • Workout and exercise logs
  • Body measurements and goals

4. Photos and Images

If you use the Progress Tracker feature, you may upload photos to track your fitness journey. These photos are:

  • Stored securely in your private account
  • Only visible to you unless you choose to share them
  • Encrypted during transmission and storage

5. Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you use the App, including feature usage, interaction patterns, and crash reports to improve our service.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, data storage, and analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • FatSecret: For food database and nutritional information. FatSecret Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI: For AI-powered food recognition features. OpenAI Privacy Policy

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's features
  • To personalize your nutrition and fitness experience
  • To calculate and display your nutritional targets
  • To facilitate social features like leaderboards (if you opt in)
  • To send important service-related notifications
  • To analyze usage patterns and fix technical issues

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party companies that help us operate the App (listed above), under strict data protection agreements.
  • Social Features: If you participate in social features, your username and selected fitness stats may be visible to friends or on leaderboards based on your privacy settings.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

Subscription and Billing

CPR Nutrition offers auto-renewing subscriptions. When you subscribe:

  • Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store
  • We do not have access to your payment card information
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device's Settings

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Data is encrypted at rest in our databases
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for analytics purposes
  • Backup copies may take up to 90 days to be fully purged

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data from Account Settings
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, use the in-app settings or contact us directly.

Children's Privacy

CPR Nutrition is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date.
