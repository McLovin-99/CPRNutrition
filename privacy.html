Introduction

CPRapp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by CPRapp.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, and password. We may also collect profile information such as your name and profile picture.

2. Health and Fitness Data

With your permission, we access data from Apple HealthKit (specifically step count) to provide fitness tracking features. We also collect data you manually enter, such as food logs, workouts, and body measurements.

3. Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the app, including feature usage and interaction data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To improve and personalize your experience.
  • To facilitate social features like leaderboards and challenges.
  • To analyze usage patterns and fix technical issues.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies (like Firebase) that help us operate the Service.
  • Other Users: Your username, profile picture, and certain fitness stats (like step count or challenge progress) may be visible to friends or on public leaderboards, depending on your privacy settings.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal data directly within the app settings or by contacting us.
